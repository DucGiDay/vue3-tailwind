# FABI CMS Design System

The FABI CMS design system is built to provide a professional, efficient, and reliable management interface. It combines the stability of enterprise software with the modern aesthetics of a contemporary SaaS application.

## Look and Feel

### Visual Identity

The system uses a **clean, structured layout** with a focus on information density and readability. The primary aesthetic is "Modern Professional," characterized by:

- **Trustworthy Color Palette**: A deep blue primary color signifies stability and professional rigor.
- **Vibrant Accents**: Use of purple for branding and high-visibility actions to prevent the UI from feeling overly clinical.
- **Soft Geometry**: Large border radii (`lg`) on inputs and containers soften the enterprise grid, making the application feel more approachable.

### Design Intent

- **Clarity First**: Heavy reliance on a semantic gray scale ensures that content hierarchy is clear and that functional colors (success, error) stand out immediately.
- **Interactive Precision**: Focus states are explicitly defined with a clear 2px ring, ensuring accessibility and providing immediate feedback during keyboard navigation.
- **Responsive Fluidity**: The system is built on a flexible grid that adapts from small screens (576px) to ultra-wide displays (1920px), maintaining component integrity throughout.

## Design Principles

1.  **Professionalism**: Every element should feel deliberate and high-quality.
2.  **Efficiency**: Minimize cognitive load by using consistent patterns and clear typography.
3.  **Reliability**: Use stable, well-tested components (PrimeVue + Tailwind) to ensure a bug-free experience.
4.  **Accessibility**: Maintain high contrast ratios and clear focus indicators across the platform.
